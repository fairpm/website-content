# Packages for WordPress

Extend WordPress with plugins, or change your look completely with themes — all from decentralized, federated sources.

## Getting started

To access the FAIR ecosystem on your WordPress site, you need to install the **FAIR Connect plugin**. This plugin replaces WordPress's centralized dependency on wordpress.org with FAIR's federated infrastructure. Once installed, your site will be able to discover, install, and update plugins and themes from both official and independent FAIR sources — directly from your WordPress dashboard, just like you're used to.

You can [download the latest version of FAIR Connect from GitHub](https://github.com/fairpm/fair-plugin/releases), or ask your hosting provider to enable FAIR for your site.
